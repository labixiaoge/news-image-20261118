## About

An entry for the make.fun competition
[Sum up 18 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261118).
The program is a one-step copy: `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only)
copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output`) as `entry.png`.

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

## The image

A newspaper front page headed **"Wednesday 18 November 2026 — The World
in One Day"**, with five stories from the day, each told by a picture and a
headline:

1. **Ukraine hits Crimean Bridge** — Ukrainian drones and missiles badly
   damaged the Kerch Bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea, collapsing
   part of its road span; Russia answered by striking two bridges in Kyiv
   and other infrastructure.
2. **Trump joins Quad summit in Manila** — President Trump arrived in the
   Philippines for the first Quad leaders' meeting in two years, ahead of
   the APEC forum and talks with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen.
3. **Iran drones target US radar in Bahrain** — Iran's Revolutionary Guard
   fired drones at a US radar system at Sheikh Isa Air Base; Bahrain and
   the US military said they were intercepted, with no damage or injuries.
4. **Cyclone Akvan bears down on India** — the storm over the Bay of Bengal
   strengthened into a severe cyclonic storm, heading for the Andhra
   Pradesh coast with landfall expected around 20 November.
5. **Dhoni retires from the IPL** — Chennai Super Kings great MS Dhoni
   ended his playing career in the IPL and stays on as the team's mentor.
